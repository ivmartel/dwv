DWV orientation check

Each row is the same synthetic volume written as a series with a different Image Orientation (Patient): all the 24 axis aligned orientations. The name is the orientation code: anatomical direction of the row, of the column and of the normal (LPS). Each series is displayed without target orientation and with an axial, coronal and sagittal one, all views at the same world position. A correct display matches the expected row: readable letters with the anatomical labels on the side they name (axial: A at the top and R on the left, coronal: S at the top and R on the left, sagittal: S at the top and A on the left). The 'no orientation' view should match the expected view of the acquisition plane.

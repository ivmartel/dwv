## Constructor

<Signature

): PageViewport"
/>

**Parameters**

- `viewBox` (Array) — xMin, yMin, xMax and yMax coordinates.
- `scale` (number) — scale of the viewport.
- `rotation` (number) — rotations of the viewport in degrees.
- `offsetX` (number) — offset X
- `offsetY` (number) — offset Y
- `dontFlip` (boolean) — if true, axis Y will not be flipped.
